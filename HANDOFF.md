 Project Handoff: Clothing Brand Wireframe

## Executive Summary

### User Problem
Fast-fashion alternatives lack high-quality, transparently sourced everyday essentials. Modern consumers struggle to find clear, distraction-free online shopping experiences that prioritize structural garment details, material origins, and transparent pricing structures over aggressive marketing pop-ups.

### Target Audience
* **Demographic:** Eco-conscious professionals, creators, and students aged 18–35.
* **Psychographic:** Values minimalist design, capsulated wardrobes, sustainable streetwear, and ethical supply chains. They look for quick navigation, reliable size guides, and straightforward digital layouts.

### Chosen Aesthetic Theme
* **Theme:** Raw Minimalist & Structured Streetwear.
* **Visual Pillars:**
  * Clean layout structure with distinct divider lines (`<hr>` markers).
  * Monochromatic palette mixed with raw, desaturated organic tones.
  * Strong typographical hierarchy, balancing structural bold grids with open white space.
